# Life of a Founder

## Overview

Life of a Founder is more than a personal portfolio.

It is a personal brand, learning platform, publishing platform, and community built around one mission:

> Rebuild your life while helping others rebuild theirs.

The website documents my journey as I become a better software engineer, writer, teacher, and founder while inviting others to learn and grow alongside me.

The project combines education, storytelling, productivity, software development, and community into one ecosystem.

---

# Core Philosophy

Most educational platforms teach isolated topics.

I want to teach the complete picture.

Imagine programming as a giant puzzle.

Most courses hand you random pieces.

I want to:

- Show every puzzle piece.
- Explain why each piece exists.
- Show how every piece connects.
- Build real projects from those pieces.

The goal is not simply to teach syntax.

The goal is to teach people how software actually works.
